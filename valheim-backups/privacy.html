Valheim Backups privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026.

Valheim Backups is a personal tool operated by its owner to keep copies of their game-server saves in their Google Drive.

Data and permissions

The tool processes game-save archives and backup-file metadata, such as filenames, sizes and checksums, to upload and verify backups. Archives may contain game-world data and in-game player identifiers.

Google authorization uses the drive.file permission, limited to files created by, or explicitly shared with, this application. The tool does not request full access to the owner's Google Drive, Gmail or contacts.

Storage and use

Backup data is processed on the owner's devices and server and stored in the Google Drive account they authorize. OAuth credentials are kept in protected configuration files on those devices and server, not published on this website or in its repository.

Data is used only to create, verify and restore backups. The tool does not sell data, use it for advertising, train machine-learning models with it or share it with other users. Google processes the files stored in Drive under its own privacy policy.

Retention and control

Cloud backups remain until the owner deletes them; the tool currently performs no automatic cloud deletion. Local copies may be rotated by the server's backup policy.

The owner can revoke the application's access through Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not delete existing backup files. The owner can remove those files directly in Google Drive.

Contact

Questions can be raised through the website repository's issue tracker. Do not post backup files, passwords or OAuth credentials there.
